Warm Glow & Sparkles

Creates a slowly moving warm red-to-orange glow along the LED strip. Brightness and color are modulated by independent waves, so the pattern moves organically instead of repeating as a single travelling wave. Short green-to-blue sparkles flash at changing positions on top of the glow.

Parameters

Glow time frequency
Controls how quickly the warm glow changes over time. Higher values make the motion faster.
Glow space frequency
Controls how densely the glow varies along the LED strip. Higher values create more spatial variation.
Flash frequency
Controls how often each sparkle group produces a flash.
Sparkle groups
Divides the strip into this many independent regions. Each flash chooses a new random LED within its region.
